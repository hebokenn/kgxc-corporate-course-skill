# C01 持机姿态修订

方式：内置 imagegen。原身份参考：C01-full-v1.png；机型参考：本次代码绘制 iPhone 18 Pro 素材板的最右侧背面。原身份确认不等于此新姿态的动作确认。

实际生成提示词：

Use case: identity-preserve. Edit target Image 1 is the approved C01 Chinese male customer illustration. Supporting Image 2 is a code-drawn iPhone 18 Pro reference board; use the RIGHTMOST blue BACK VIEW as phone-design reference, do not reproduce the board. Create ONE production full-body transparent cutout of the SAME character naturally using an iPhone 18 Pro. Preserve his exact face identity, brown hair, blue denim jacket with stitching, white T-shirt, dark trousers, gray-and-white sneakers, cartoon illustration style, body proportions and clothing textures. Change ONLY pose, head orientation and hands as needed. He is standing naturally in a slight three-quarter view, head tilted gently down, eyes clearly looking at the phone in his hands, calm focused closed-mouth expression. Phone held vertically at lower chest level with screen facing HIM; the audience sees the ICE BLUE BACK of the phone, including its broad top camera plateau and three cameras. Both elbows bent naturally close to his body. Left hand supports the phone from its lower left edge and base; fingers curl naturally around its SIDE, not palms raised toward the audience. Right hand holds the other side with the right thumb reaching the screen on the FAR side, mostly hidden from the audience. Realistic coherent cartoon anatomy: normal sized hands, five fingers per hand, aligned wrists, believable grip contact and occlusion. No large splayed hands, no palms stuck on the display, no floating phone, no audience-facing screen. The phone has small realistic scale relative to the head and hands, not a tablet. Draw the hands carefully and the device as distinct clean shapes for later code compositing. Full body from hair to shoe soles, centered, completely visible with generous transparent margins. True transparent background, no environment, no floor, no vignette, no drop shadow, no text, no labels, no extra objects. Output one portrait-format transparent PNG, high detail at the same illustration quality as Image 1.

生成图上的手机只作接触代理。实际工作台在自己的平面变换上绘制具名机型，再以该图自身分离的手部作前景遮挡，保留人物原有颜色与接触位置。修改手机改 device_models.py 或普通节点；修改手势/视角须重制该角色接触姿态并重标，不能只改 transform 伪造握持。
